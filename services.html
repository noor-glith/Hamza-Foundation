Our services

Affordable care for the whole family

Call before you visit to check that the service you need is running that day.

  • General Medicine

    Check-ups and treatment for everyday illnesses such as fever, cough, infections and aches, by medical officers in the outpatient department.

  • Specialist Consultants

    Consultants of different specialties see patients at a heavily subsidised fee. Call to ask which consultant is available on which day.

  • Diabetes & Hypertension

    Regular follow-up for diabetes and high blood pressure, including medicine review and diet advice.

  • Gynecology

    Care for women's health, including pregnancy check-ups, seen by woman medical officers.

  • Child Care & Vaccination

    Check-ups for babies and children, treatment of common childhood illnesses, and routine vaccinations.

  • Dentistry

    Dental check-ups and treatment for toothache, cleaning and fillings.

  • Pathology Lab

    Blood, urine and other tests at the main pathology laboratory of Hamza Foundation, located at this centre.

    At this centre

  • Pharmacy

    Medicines from the main drug store of Hamza Foundation, located at this centre.

    At this centre

Inside the dental clinic

Qualified dentists see patients for check-ups, cleaning, fillings and treatment of toothache, using modern dental chairs and equipment.
