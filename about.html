About the hospital

Serving Samanabad since 1978

Our story

Amin Hayat Memorial Medical Center in Pakki Thatti is the flagship medical centre of Hamza Foundation. It was established in 1978 and has the largest team of any Hamza Foundation centre.

Specialists, consultants, medical officers, woman medical officers and paramedical staff see patients here at a heavily subsidised fee, with free care for deserving patients. An evening shift runs alongside the morning OPD.

Part of Hamza Foundation

  • Five medical centres

    Amin Hayat Memorial Medical Centers run in Lahore, Kasur, Hussain Khanwala and Shershah, Karachi.

  • Main lab and drug store

    This centre houses the foundation's main pathology laboratory and main drug store.

  • More than healthcare

    Hamza Foundation also runs the Academy for the Deaf and a school in Lahore.
